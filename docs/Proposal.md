# LIKSI: Listing Intentions, Keeping Schedules In-check 

## Brief Description:
LIKSI is a program that specializes in keeping your schedule in tact. It helps in aiding you to realize what needs your immediate attention when the deadline is looming near and also organizes your upcoming requirements by priority.

## Description:
Specific features of the organizement of the requirement includes the ones that has a heavy toll in your grade or the deadline is near at the top; e.g. AAs, presentations, Laboratory reports, and other such requirements. For some that still affects your grade but is far enough to have more flexibility, it is situated at the bottom. LIKSI also provides reminders and optimal times to do requirements based on the schedule that you provide yourself. 

## Problem Statement: 
When your schedule is as busy as a Pisay scholar, there may be times where you forget to do the requirements you have to pass; especially when it is a week wherein the periodical examinations are fast approaching and projects need to be completed and requirements need to be passed — amidst all that, there may be things you took note of but forgot. LIKSI provides constant reminders without prompting and also automatically organizes requirements based on the grading system set in that subject. The application is needed because instead of you having to get reminders for everything and getting notifications for things that you need to do on the day of the deadline — LIKSI makes sure to optimize your time and also allots schedules for you to do requirements. 

## Problem Identified:
When schedules are ever packed and things seem to be moving at a faster pace every time, you take note of requirements that you slowly forget due to the passing of time. It can’t be avoided, especially when there are new ones showing up when something just finished. 

## Target Users / Beneficiaries:
The target users/beneficiaries are the people that know themselves to often forget things especially with high stakes and a lot of events going on that causes them to divide their focus, and because of that division, they may not provide enough for something and it can really affect their performance academically, especially when that something is very important to them.

---

* Primary Users: Scholars with busy schedules, Students participating in extracurricular activities
* Secondary Users / Beneficiaries: Teachers who keeps track of students submitting requirements on time and less reminders, GWAs that grow due to more optimized schedules for studying and submissions

## Outline of Features

| Feature | Description | Persistent Data Used |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Feature 1 | Organizes requirements by priority and deadlines, considering their impact on the student's grades and urgency. |Task details, priority levels, due dates, subject grading weights, and completion status | 
| Feature 2 | Provides automatic reminders for upcoming deadlines and unfinished requirements. | Task deadlines, reminder settings, notification schedules, and completion status |
| Feature 3 | Suggests optimal schedules and time slots for completing requirements based on the student's provided schedule. | Student's personal schedule, available time slots, task durations, and deadlines |
| Feature 4 | Automatically updates and reorganizes the priority of requirements as deadlines approach and new tasks are added. | Existing task records, newly added requirements, updated deadlines, priority levels, and completion status |

---

## Description of Data to be Collected and Stored as Persistent Data (Optional) 
| Data Field | Description | Purpose/Use | 
|:---|:---|:---|
| Student ID | Unique Log-In Identifier of the user | Used for logging in and account management |
| Objective Name | Name of the assigned objective | Used to display and manage objectives |
| Student Schedule | School schedule of the user | Used to assign priorities to objectives and provide insight to the user on what steps to take |
| Grading System | Per unit system of students grades | Used to guide in prioritizing based on per unit value/weight of the objectives per subject |
| Modes of Participation | The availability of other individuals around the user and modality | Used to identify whether an objective deserves higher priority based on the availability of other individuals around the user, or whether the objective is group-based. |
